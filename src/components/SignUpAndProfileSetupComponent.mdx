import {ADRI_URLS} from '@site/src/constants/urls';

### Step 1: Sign Up on Adri

1. Open <a href={ADRI_URLS.signup}>getadri.ai/signup</a>.
2. Click **Sign up** (if needed) and complete registration.
3. If you already have an account, use <a href={ADRI_URLS.login}>getadri.ai/login</a>.

**Done when:** You are prompted to verify your email.

![Step 1 placeholder: highlight Sign up top-right](https://res.cloudinary.com/diufkmalk/image/upload/v1773578385/Screenshot_2026-03-15_at_6.04.35_PM_svvitq.png)

### Step 2: Verify Email and Complete Profile

1. Open the verification email and click the verification link.
2. Fill in your basic details.
3. Submit the form.

**Done when:** You land on the **Projects** page.
