Why Executives Should Care About Context Instrumentation

April 19, 2026 · 2 min read
Shyam Swaroop
Co-Founder & CTO, Adri AI

AI in SAP is often discussed in technical terms, but the real story is business impact. This is about speed, cost, and scalability.

From months to days​

Integrations​

  • Bank and logistics integrations
    • Before: months
    • Now: around 1 day

Regulatory changes​

  • E-invoicing and compliance updates
    • Before: months
    • Now: around 1 day

Operational automation​

  • Routine workflows
    • Before: around 1 week
    • Now: around 1 day

This is not small optimization. This is cycle-time collapse.

Path to ROI​

Month 1: about 25% efficiency gain​

  • Faster debugging
  • Early automation wins

Month 3: about 50% efficiency gain​

  • Larger features delivered faster
  • Increased confidence in change delivery

Month 6: about 80% efficiency gain​

  • End-to-end workflow acceleration
  • Agent supports build, test, and documentation

Year 1 outcome​

With the same team size, organizations can deliver significantly more applications.

Strategic value​

Faster automation​

Complex processes become continuously improvable.

Faster application delivery​

Backlogs shrink and innovation speeds up.

Built-in quality​

Quality improves through repeatable checks and stronger test discipline.

Cost reduction​

Teams can reduce SaaS dependency and build more capabilities internally at lower cost.

Executive takeaway​

This is not only a developer productivity tool. It is a business transformation engine that improves time-to-market, quality, and agility.

Final thought​

Most transformation initiatives promise speed. Few truly remove bottlenecks.

Context instrumentation does exactly that.

Once this shift happens, the question is no longer "Can we build this?" It becomes:

What should we build next?
